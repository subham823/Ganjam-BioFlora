ABOUT THE PROJECT

Ganjam's Plant Diversity

Ganjam BioFlora is designed as a biotechnology learning and research platform focused on documented plant diversity of Ganjam, Odisha.

The platform will connect plants with taxonomy, traditional uses, phytochemicals, research literature and biotechnology applications.
